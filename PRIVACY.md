# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Calendar Wall is a personal, self-hosted hobby project. It runs on hardware you
control and is not offered as a hosted service.

## Google data

When you connect a Google account, the app requests read-only access to:

- Google Calendar events and calendar names
- Google Tasks lists and items
- Photos you choose in the Google Photos Picker

This data is used only to display your calendar, tasks, and selected photos on
your own wall display. The OAuth refresh token, cached Google responses, and
imported photos are stored on the machine running the app. Nothing is sent to
the developer or to any third party, and nothing is sold, shared, or used for
advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other services

The app fetches weather from Open-Meteo using the latitude and longitude you
configure, and a daily quote from ZenQuotes. No Google data is sent to them.

## Removing data

Revoke access at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>. Deleting the app's
data volume on your machine removes the stored token, cache, and photos.
